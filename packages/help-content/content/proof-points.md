# Proof & pricing

Your agent never makes up numbers. That's deliberate — a fabricated stat or a fake customer story is
the fastest way to lose a prospect's trust. But it means that when someone asks *"can you prove it?"*
or *"what does it cost?"* and you haven't given your agent any real facts, it can only say it doesn't
have that answer. On a warm conversation, that can stall a deal right at the finish line.

Proof points fix that. They're the true facts you let your agent share when a prospect asks.

## What to add

- **Stat / metric** — a real number you can stand behind. *"42% average show rate on booked calls."*
- **Result / case study** — a real outcome, usually anonymized. *"A fintech client booked 12 qualified calls in their first month."*
- **Pricing** — a real number or range. *"Starts at $2k/mo, usually $2–4k depending on volume."*
- **FAQ answer** — the honest answer to a question you get asked a lot, paired with the question it answers.

## How your agent uses them

- Only **when it's relevant** — a prospect asks for evidence or price, or a fact genuinely strengthens
  the point. Your agent won't dump stats into a message or lead with them.
- **Never in a first message.** The opening touch stays a short, human question — proof is for the
  conversation that follows.
- **Quoted as written, never embellished.** Your agent uses your exact fact and won't stretch it into a
  bigger claim. If a prospect asks for something you haven't added, it says it doesn't have that number
  rather than guessing.

## The one rule

These are sent to prospects **as fact, on your behalf**. Add only true, verifiable statements. Use
anonymized references like *"a fintech client"* unless you have permission to name a customer. Good
proof points are honest, specific, and short — one quotable line each.

Only workspace admins can add or remove proof points, since they change what every conversation is
allowed to claim. Manage them under **Settings › Proof & pricing**.
