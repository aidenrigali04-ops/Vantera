# Send modes: review vs automatic

Your Outreach Agent has two send modes that control whether drafted messages go to a review queue before sending or send automatically. You can switch modes on the agent's settings page.

## Review every draft (default)

Every draft your Outreach Agent produces lands in the review queue before anything is sent. You read, edit if needed, and approve. This is the recommended mode when you're first getting started — it lets you see what the agent produces and build confidence before letting it run on its own.

## Automatic

Clean drafts send without requiring your approval. The agent handles the queue on its own, and outreach goes out at a natural, human-like pace throughout each day.

**One safety valve always applies:** any draft that has style flags — signs the copy may sound too templated or may not meet quality standards — is held for your review regardless of mode. Automatic mode only skips the queue for drafts that pass cleanly. You'll never have flagged copy go out without a chance to look at it.

## Pacing and daily limits

In both modes, approved or automatic sends are spaced throughout the day rather than batched all at once. This applies to both email and LinkedIn:

- **Email**: each mailbox has a per-day cap that scales with its warm-up stage.
- **LinkedIn**: connection invites ramp up gradually on newer accounts and stay under approximately 100 per week. This ceiling protects your account from platform restrictions.

These limits are set by Vantera and cannot be raised — they're there to keep your accounts healthy over the long term, not to throttle you arbitrarily.

## Where to switch

Open your Outreach Agent's settings and look for **Send mode**. Changes take effect on the next draft cycle.
