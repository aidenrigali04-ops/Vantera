# Your outreach sequence

The sequence page, titled **The flow every prospect runs**, sets the LinkedIn touches every qualified prospect gets: a connection request, then personalized follow-ups. It stops the moment a prospect books a meeting. Open it from **Configure sequence** on the Pipeline tab of Results.

You need a deployed Outreach Agent before the sequence runs. Until then the page says "Deploy your Outreach Agent to activate this sequence — you can still tune it here." and links to Playbook.

## The settings

The LinkedIn card has three numbers and a switch:

- **Touches** — how many LinkedIn touches each prospect gets, the connection request and its follow-ups, from 0 to 5. The default is 2.
- **Gap (days)** — the days between one touch and the next, from 0 to 14.
- **Wait then advance** — the days to wait after the last touch before the sequence moves on, from 0 to 14.
- **Enabled** — turns the LinkedIn sequence on or off.

Press **Save sequence** to keep your changes. You don't set message wording here: the Outreach Agent writes each message for that person, and no touches are used up while a connection request is still waiting to be accepted.

## The safety limits

Under the settings, one line states the limits every send stays within:

"Sends stay within LinkedIn-safe limits: 20 invites a day once ramped (5 → 10 → 15 over the first four weeks), 25 messages a day, about 100 invites a week."

These are the same limits the system enforces, read from it directly, so what you see is what applies. A newly connected account starts small and ramps up over its first four weeks. You can't raise them: they protect your own LinkedIn account. A longer sequence never sends faster. It only means more touches per person, paced within these limits.
