# Using the help copilot

The help copilot is an in-platform assistant available on every dashboard page. Ask it questions, and it will answer from Vantera's built-in knowledge — no searching through docs.

**What it can do:**

- **Answer questions** about how any feature works, from setting up your first agent to reviewing drafted outreach.
- **Guide you step by step** — ask "how do I do X?" and it can walk you through the screen with a highlighted walkthrough, pointing out each control as you go.
- **Take actions for you** — things like pausing an agent, updating your revenue goal, or navigating to a specific page. It always tells you what it is about to do and asks for your confirmation before changing anything.

**What it never does:**

- It will not change billing settings or initiate any outreach on your behalf. For billing, it will point you to the right page.
- It will not send messages or make changes without your explicit go-ahead — every mutating action requires a confirmation step.

**How to use it:**

Open the copilot panel from the help button on any dashboard page. Type your question in plain language. If the copilot suggests an action, review what it proposes, then confirm or cancel — you are always in control.
