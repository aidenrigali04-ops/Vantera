# Meetings

**Meetings** is where every booked meeting your outreach produced lives — the number the whole
system works for.

## How a meeting gets recorded

- **Detected from the reply** — when a prospect's message confirms a scheduled meeting, Vera
  records it automatically.
- **Marked by you** — on any prospect's page, use **Mark meeting booked** (with an optional time).
  Your manual mark is always authoritative.

Either way, booking is treated as a real event: queued outreach to that prospect is canceled and
the sequence stops — someone who just booked never gets another scripted nudge. You'll see a
notification the moment it happens.

## Your booking link matters most

Vera offers your booking link the moment a prospect shows interest — it's how conversations
become meetings. Set it during onboarding or anytime in your Outreach agent's settings. If it's
missing, the dashboard shows an action card until it's set.

## From booked to closed

A booked meeting isn't a closed deal. When the deal lands, use **Mark closed-won** on the prospect's
page (with the real deal value) — that's what moves revenue and pushes the win to your CRM.
