# Positioning

Your **positioning** is how your agent represents you: the value proposition it uses in conversations, the voice it writes in, and the lines it must never cross. Set it in **Settings › Positioning**, and confirm your value proposition during onboarding (we pre-fill it from your website so you're editing a sentence, not writing from scratch).

## Value proposition

One or two sentences on what you do, for whom, and why it's worth their time. Your agent draws on this when a prospect asks what you do. If you leave it blank, your agent uses what we read from your website.

## Brand voice

A short description of the tone your messages should match — for example, "warm, direct" or "hospitality-industry insider." It shapes *how* your agent writes, not *what* it claims.

## Guardrails

Hard limits your agent will never cross, one per line — for example, "Never claim we're SOC 2 certified." Your agent honors these in every message.

## The cold connection request stays un-pitched

Your positioning shapes the **conversation** — the replies and follow-ups once a prospect engages. The first connection request is deliberately kept un-pitched (no product pitch, no ask), because a genuine, low-pressure first touch is what earns the reply. Your positioning does the selling once the conversation is live.
