# Plans and billing

Vantera offers three plans — **Starter**, **Growth**, and **Scale** — each with a set of included seats and usage limits. Your plan determines how many teammates can log in and how much outreach capacity you get by default.

## Add-ons

LinkedIn accounts and extra seats are available as add-ons, billed per unit on top of your base plan. You can add or remove these at any time from the Billing page; changes take effect immediately and are prorated on your next invoice.

## Choosing a plan and paying

From the Billing page, select the plan that fits your team. You'll be taken to a secure checkout to enter your payment details. Once payment is confirmed, your workspace is upgraded instantly and new limits apply right away.

## Updating payment details or changing your plan

Go to **Settings → Billing** and open the billing portal. From there you can:

- **Update your card or payment method** — changes apply to future invoices.
- **Upgrade or downgrade your plan** — upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period.
- **Cancel your subscription** — cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period. Your workspace stays active until then.

## What happens if a payment fails

If a payment fails, Vantera will retry over the following days and notify you by email. During that window:

- **New outreach pauses** — no new messages are sent while the account is past due.
- **You cannot add new resources** (extra seats, LinkedIn accounts) until the balance is resolved.
- **Your existing data is safe** — contacts, campaigns, and history are preserved.

Once payment is resolved or a new payment method is added, outreach resumes automatically and all features are restored. If your subscription lapses entirely, reactivating it restores full access to your data.
