# Approvals — approving drafts before they send

Every message your Outreach Agent writes waits in **Approvals** — nothing sends without your approval, unless you have switched to Autopilot (see below).

## What you see

Approvals has two panes. On the left is the **queue**: one row per draft, with the prospect, the kind of message, and why they were chosen. On the right is the **open draft**: the prospect's fit score with three tier bars, their title and company, the why-now evidence with a source link, and the message itself. You approve with the same context the agent had.

LinkedIn outreach goes out in two steps, so drafts come in pairs: a **connection request** and a **Follow-up** message. Each is tagged so you know which is which. The draft is shown the way the prospect will see it: an invitation note with a character counter against the LinkedIn limit, or a message as a chat bubble. Press **E** to edit it in place.

## Working a draft

- **Approve** (**A**) — the draft is cleared to send.
- **Edit** (**E**) — change the message. The style check re-runs automatically.
- **Regenerate** (**G**) — the agent writes a fresh version. Type a note in the box first to steer it, for example "Make it shorter, lead with the hiring signal" (up to 280 characters), or leave it empty for a new take. Regenerate works on drafts the agent wrote as part of a sequence (including follow-ups); reply and hand-written drafts show why it's off.
- **Reject** (**R**) — drop the draft. Pick a quick reason (*Wrong person*, *Bad timing*, *Weak message*, or *Other*); rejections teach your agent who and what to avoid.
- **Decline & never contact** — in the draft's overflow menu. It drops the draft and adds the contact to your suppression list so no agent messages them again.

Approve and reject show a 5-second **Undo**. Changed your mind in that window? One click puts the draft back.

## Regenerate or Fix

If our style check spots anything that could read as robotic, the draft shows a flag and a **Fix** button. Fix rewrites the draft without changing what it says, then re-checks it against the same style rules, so a flag only goes away when the text genuinely passes. You can also edit it yourself. **Fix** only appears when there is something to fix. Regenerate is different: it is a fresh draft, steered by your note if you wrote one.

## Keys

**A** approve · **E** edit · **G** regenerate · **R** reject · **←** / **→** previous or next draft · **Esc** leaves a mode (editing, rejecting) · **?** shows the key hint (Approvals only). On Today, **A**, **E**, **G**, **R** and **←** / **→** work in the open draft.

## Autopilot or Review

The switch at the top of Approvals sets your send mode (the same one as on your Outreach Agent in Playbook; see the send modes article).

- **Review** — every draft waits here for you.
- **Autopilot** — clean drafts send on their own at a human-like pace. Switching to it asks you to confirm first. Flagged drafts still wait in Approvals: flagged copy never sends without your eyes on it.

## When sending is blocked

If something stops messages from going out, a stack of notices appears above the list, each with a button to fix it: LinkedIn disconnected or restricted, a failed payment, no LinkedIn account connected, or everything paused. Drafts keep queuing meanwhile, so nothing is lost.

## After you approve

Approved messages don't fire all at once. They schedule at a natural, human-like pace and then send, which protects your LinkedIn account. Switch to the **Processed** tab to follow them.

Each prospect appears once, with their full conversation underneath: the exact message your agent sent on your behalf, and any reply that came back (tagged *Interested*, *Not interested*, and so on). Every message carries its status:

- **Approved** — cleared, waiting to be scheduled.
- **Scheduled** — queued to send at a specific time.
- **Sent** — delivered.
- **Failed** — something went wrong; the reason shows on the message.
- **Canceled** — stopped before sending (for example, an invite that was never accepted).
- **Suppressed** — the contact is on your suppression list, so the message was held back.

On Autopilot, the **Processed** tab shows exactly what went out: the full copy of every message and the replies it earned, so automatic never means invisible.

When you've worked through everything, the queue tells you you're all caught up and points you to the Inbox and to what's sending next.
