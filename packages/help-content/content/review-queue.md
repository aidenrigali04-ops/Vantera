# Reviewing drafts before they send

Every message your Outreach Agent writes waits in the review queue — nothing sends without your approval.

Each draft shows who it's for, the channel, and the message. If our style check spotted anything that could read as robotic, you'll see a flag on the card; edit the draft and the flag clears once the text passes.

- **Approve** — the draft is cleared to send.
- **Edit** — change the subject or message; we re-check the style automatically.
- **Decline** — drop this draft.
- **Decline & never contact** — drop the draft and add the contact to your suppression list so no agent messages them again.

LinkedIn outreach goes out in two steps, so its drafts come in pairs: an **Invite** (the connection note) and a **Follow-up** (the first message, which only sends after the prospect accepts). Each card is tagged so you know which is which.

## After you approve

Approved messages don't fire all at once. They schedule at a natural, human-like pace and then send — this protects your sending reputation and your LinkedIn account. Switch to the **Processed** tab to watch them move through their stages:

- **Approved** → cleared, waiting to be scheduled.
- **Scheduled** → queued to send at a specific time.
- **Sent** → delivered.
- **Failed** → something went wrong; the reason shows on the row.
- **Canceled** → stopped before sending (for example, a LinkedIn invite that was never accepted).
- **Suppressed** → the contact was added to your suppression list, so the message was held back.

If your Outreach Agent is set to **automatic** sending, clean drafts skip the queue and land straight in **Processed** as Approved — anything with a style flag still comes to you first.
