# Inbox

**Inbox** shows every conversation your outreach is having — both sides of the thread, in one
place. Unanswered warm replies are pinned to the top so the person waiting on you is always
first.

## The thread

Each thread shows everything: the messages Vera sent (labeled with the play that wrote them),
anything you sent yourself, and every reply from the prospect with its read on it (interested,
not interested, and so on).

## Replying

- **Send** — queues your message through the normal, account-safe send path.
- **Draft with Vera** — Vera writes the next message for this exact thread, grounded in the
  conversation so far and your proof points. You edit, then send. If a queued draft already
  exists, the composer opens with it — one click to send.
- **Let Vera handle it** — hands the whole thread back to automation: Vera's reply is queued
  (through your review queue unless the agent is in automatic mode) and follow-ups resume.

Replying yourself pauses automation on that thread — Vera never messages on top of you. Hand it
back anytime with "Let Vera handle it."

You can also message any lead directly from their profile page — even before they've replied.
Prospects who opted out can never be messaged again.
