# Team and seats

Your workspace can include multiple teammates, each with their own login. How many people can join is determined by the seats included in your plan, plus any extra seats you've added.

## Seats vs. LinkedIn accounts

- A **seat** is a person who can log in to your workspace. Seats are about who can access and manage the platform.
- **LinkedIn accounts** are outreach channels — the accounts that send connection requests and messages. These are separate from seats and are managed independently.

## Inviting teammates

Go to **Settings → Team** and enter your teammate's email address. Choose a role:

- **Member** — can view and operate campaigns but cannot change workspace settings, manage billing, or invite or remove other team members.
- **Admin** — has full access: all member permissions plus the ability to manage team members, billing, and workspace settings.

An invitation email is sent to the address you entered. The invite remains pending until your teammate accepts it by clicking the link and completing sign-in. Pending invites appear in the team list and can be revoked before they are accepted. Invites expire after 7 days — an expired invite is marked in the list, and **Resend** gives it a fresh 7-day window and sends the email again.

## Accepting an invitation

Click the link in the email — the invite page shows which workspace you're joining and the address the invite was sent to. If you already have a Vantera account, sign in (Google or email) and you'll return to the invite automatically. If you're new, choose **Create your account & join**: you'll sign up with the invited email (no company setup — the workspace already exists) and land directly on your team's dashboard. **Continue with Google** works here too, but it must be the Google account for that same invited address.

## Revoking access

Admins and the workspace owner can remove any team member from **Settings → Team**. Removing someone immediately revokes their access — they can no longer log in to the workspace, but no data they created is deleted.

## Workspace owner

The workspace owner is the account that created the workspace. The owner cannot be removed from the team. To transfer ownership, contact support.

## Seat limits

If you've reached your seat limit, you'll see a notice when you try to invite someone. You can add extra seats from the Billing page at any time, and the new seat is available immediately.
