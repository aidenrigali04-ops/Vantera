# Learning

**Learning** is off until this workspace has a subject. Open **Learning** and create the first one, or have the first one created from the workspace name. Outreach does not change when you do that.

A subject is the thing this account is learning. There is one per workspace. Naming it does not choose a message, a target, or a strategy.

## What stays the same

People are still contacted the way they were before the subject existed. A test that would contact someone differently waits in **Approvals** until you approve it, unless you turned on “Start approved tests without asking.”

## Pause

**Pause learning** stops the subject. Outreach continues. **Resume learning** turns the subject back on.
