# Understanding your Results dashboard

**Results** is the deeper view behind Today — open it from search (⌘K) when you want the full picture of what's working. It has three tabs along the top: **Overview** (revenue, goal pace, live agents, top prospects, recent replies), **Analytics** (counts first: the eight-stage funnel from Found to Closed and where your prospects come from, then return on spend once you've set a deal size and have a plan), and **Pipeline** (the live, stage-by-stage view of every prospect your agents are working).

On the **Overview** tab:

- **Your goal** — the ICP, industry, and monthly revenue goal you set during onboarding. Everything Vantera does is measured against this. Edit it in Settings.
- **Revenue** — closed and expected monthly revenue against your goal. *Closed* uses the real deal values you type when you mark a prospect closed-won (falling back to your average value per client when a deal has no typed value); *Expected* is the prospects still in your pipeline (qualified, in outreach, replied) weighted by how close each stage is to closing. These two numbers mean the same thing everywhere — the Pipeline tab's "In pipeline" is this same expected value, and its "Revenue progress" is this same closed value. Set your **average value per client** in Settings to see dollar figures — until then the card shows closed-client and pipeline counts. In your first days — before anything has closed and while pipeline is still small — the card shows your four real firsts instead of the goal chart (first message sent, first warm reply, first meeting booked, first client closed); the chart takes over as pipeline builds.
- **Getting started** — your setup checklist. Launching your first campaign and getting your first reply unlock as those features arrive.
- **Prospect Agent** — your agent's status. *Standing by* means it's ready and waiting for a campaign; it flips to *Live* when one launches.
- **Hot leads** — your strongest-fit prospects to work today. The top prospect is spotlighted with its live **buying signal** (the "why now"), what a client like this is worth toward your goal, and the one next action — if a draft is already waiting for that person, the card links you straight to Approvals. The rest queue below it, each led by its own signal. Prospects marked **In-market** were surfaced by your Intent Agent because they're showing buying behavior on LinkedIn right now — engaging your competitors or posting about your space.
- **Vera is learning** — which buyer segment is being prioritized based on your actual outcomes, and the version of your playbook in use. Every line is real data from your account — click through to Analytics for the full funnel.
- **Warm replies** — interested replies that are still **waiting on your answer**. Once a reply has been answered (by you or by the agent) it leaves this list, so the count always matches reality. Not-interested replies never appear here — they show in the notification bell and stop the sequence automatically.

**Meetings** in the sidebar lists every booked meeting your outreach produced — detected from
replies or marked by you on the prospect's page. Booking stands outreach down automatically.

**Notifications** (the bell in the left rail) collects your prospect events — an interested reply, a booked meeting, a fresh buying signal, a thread that needs you. Clicking a notification takes you straight to that prospect's page. Unread events are highlighted; opening the bell marks them read.

**Prospects** in the sidebar is your daily opportunity feed — sourced and scored prospects, with the hottest ones pulled to the top. **Analytics** and **Pipeline** are now tabs here on Results rather than separate sidebar pages.
