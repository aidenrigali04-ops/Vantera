# Understanding your Results dashboard

**Results** is your home base — one place that proves the system is working. It has three tabs along the top: **Overview** (revenue, goal pace, live agents, top prospects, recent replies), **Analytics** (the conversion funnel and return on spend), and **Pipeline** (the live, stage-by-stage view of every lead your agents are working).

On the **Overview** tab:

- **Your goal** — the ICP, industry, and monthly revenue goal you set during onboarding. Everything Vantera does is measured against this. Edit it in Settings.
- **Revenue** — closed and expected monthly revenue against your goal. *Closed* is your converted clients multiplied by your average value per client; *Expected* is the leads still in your pipeline (qualified, in outreach, replied) weighted by how close each stage is to closing. Set your **average value per client** in Settings to see dollar figures — until then the card shows closed-client and pipeline counts.
- **Getting started** — your setup checklist. Launching your first campaign and getting your first reply unlock as those features arrive.
- **SDR Prospect Agent** — your agent's status. *Standing by* means it's ready and waiting for a campaign; it flips to *Live* when one launches.

**Leads** in the sidebar is your daily opportunity feed — sourced and scored prospects, with the hottest ones pulled to the top. **Analytics** and **Pipeline** are now tabs here on Results rather than separate sidebar pages.
