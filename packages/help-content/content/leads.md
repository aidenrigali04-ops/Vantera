# Your leads and how they're scored

The Leads page shows every prospect your Prospect Agent sourced. Each lead goes through two checks: a rules gate that confirms basic fit with your ideal customer profile, and an AI ranking that scores nuanced fit from 0–100 with a written rationale.

Each lead carries a fit verdict at a glance — Hot lead, Strong fit, or Worth a look — based on its score, so you can spot the best prospects without reading every number.

**Hot right now** sits at the top of the page: your strongest-fit leads, pulled out of the list so you can work the best opportunities first. Every lead also shows a one-line **why now** — a real buying signal captured during enrichment (a funding round, an executive hire, a new office, a product launch, or active buying intent) when we have one, otherwise the AI's read on the lead's top trigger or pain. A real, dated signal always wins, because it's the difference between a guess and insider timing. When a lead has replied but the reply has been sitting for a few days, it's marked **cooling** so a warm conversation never quietly goes cold.

Click any lead to open its profile. At the top you'll see what closing that prospect is worth to you (based on your average deal value) and how many deals of that size it takes to reach your revenue goal. Below that, "Your opening" gives you the angle most likely to land and the "aha moment" your outreach can build on, followed by why they're a fit — pain points, buying triggers, and motivations — and verified contact details. Leads that didn't fit are under "Filtered out", each with the reasons.

When a newly qualified lead carries a fresh, high-value signal — a funding round, an executive hire, an acquisition, or active buying intent — you'll get a **hot-signal notification** in the bell, so you can pounce while the timing is right.

Only high-quality leads (score 70+ by default) move on to outreach drafting. Prospects that never qualify are automatically removed after 90 days.
