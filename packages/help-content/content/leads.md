# Your prospects and how they're scored

The Prospects page shows every prospect your Prospect Agent sourced. Each prospect goes through two checks: a rules gate that confirms basic fit with your ideal customer profile, and an AI ranking that scores nuanced fit from 0–100 with a written rationale.

Each prospect carries a fit verdict at a glance — Hot prospect, Strong fit, or Worth a look — based on its score, so you can spot the best ones without reading every number.

**Hot right now** sits at the top of the page: your strongest-fit prospects, pulled out of the list so you can work the best opportunities first. The table gives every prospect its own **Why now** column — a real buying signal captured during enrichment (a funding round, an executive hire, a new office, a product launch, or active buying intent) when we have one, otherwise the AI's read on the prospect's top trigger or pain. A real, dated signal always wins, because it's the difference between a guess and insider timing. When a prospect has replied but the reply has been sitting for a few days, it's marked **cooling** in the same column so a warm conversation never quietly goes cold. On wider screens you'll also see **Worth** — what closing that prospect means toward your revenue goal — and **Last activity**, the most recent thing that happened (a reply, a research pass, or sourcing).

You can **search** the list by name, company, or title, and narrow it with **Filters** — industry, minimum score, when the prospect was sourced, or in-market only. Sort by **Score**, **Company**, or **Last activity** (click a column header; click again to go back to newest first), choose how many prospects show per page (10, 25, or 50), and jump straight to any page. The table also remembers your comfortable/compact row-density choice.

To work many prospects at once, tick the **checkboxes** and use the bulk bar: **Archive** stops outreach to the selected prospects and cancels their queued drafts (history kept), and **Suppress** permanently adds them to your never-contact list — both ask you to confirm first.

Click any prospect to open a **quick side panel** — the score and rationale, why-now signal, worth toward your goal, pain points and buying triggers, and their LinkedIn profile — without losing your place in the list. From there, **Open full profile** takes you to the complete brief: "Your opening" with the angle most likely to land, the full activity timeline, and deal controls. Prospects that didn't fit are under "Filtered out", each with the reasons.

When a newly qualified prospect carries a fresh, high-value signal — a funding round, an executive hire, an acquisition, or active buying intent — you'll get a **hot-signal notification** in the bell, so you can pounce while the timing is right.

## Adding a prospect yourself

**Add prospect** (top right) lets you bring your own prospect: first name and their LinkedIn profile URL are required; title and company are optional but help the scoring judge fit. They enter the exact same funnel as sourced prospects — Vera researches and scores them against your ICP, and only a qualifying score moves them into outreach drafting. Adding someone by hand is never a shortcut past the quality bar, so a hand-added prospect can still land in "Filtered out" with the reasons. Profiles on your suppression list can't be added, and a profile that's already a prospect won't be duplicated. Adding prospects is an admin action.

## Correcting a prospect

On a prospect's full profile, the pencil next to their name lets you **edit** their first/last name, title, and company — useful when research got something wrong or a prospect changed roles. Corrected details feed the next drafted message directly. Editing is admin-only.

## Notes

The **Notes** panel on the full profile keeps your own knowledge with the prospect — context from a call, a mutual connection, a reason to wait. Any teammate can read and add notes; each note shows its author and date, and you can remove your own. Notes are for your team — they're never sent to the prospect.

Only high-quality prospects (score 70+ by default) move on to outreach drafting. Prospects that never qualify are automatically removed after 90 days.
