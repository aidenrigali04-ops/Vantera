# Getting started with Vantera

When you create your account, Vantera asks four things:

1. **Your company name and website** — the website is optional (leave it blank if you don't have one). When you give one, Vantera scans it to learn what you sell — your offerings, value propositions, and the space you operate in — and shows you a summary of what it learned. Your Prospect Agent uses this to find leads that actually fit what you offer.
2. **Your industry** — so your agent prospects in the right space.
3. **Your target audience** (your ideal customer profile) — who your agents look for.
4. **Your monthly revenue goal** — your pipeline and results are tracked against this number.

Everything you enter is stored privately in your own workspace and is never shared with other customers. You can change any of these later in **Settings → Workspace**.

## Go live in one click

When you finish onboarding, hit **Go live**. From your answers, Vantera sets up your whole system at once — no wizards, nothing to deploy by hand:

- a **Prospect Agent** that sources and qualifies people who match your target audience,
- an **Outreach Agent** that writes a personal message for each one, and
- an **Intent Agent** that watches LinkedIn for people showing they're in-market — its watchlist auto-filled from your business.

It starts working within about 15 minutes, and your dashboard shows it sourcing your first leads. **Nothing sends until you approve it.** You can fine-tune any of it — your outreach call-to-action, targeting, intent watchlist, content — anytime from the **Agents** page.
