# Getting started with Vantera

Onboarding is built to get you to your first prospects fast — most of what your agent needs is read from your website, not typed in.

1. **Personalize your account** — your company name, your role, your website, and (optionally) your LinkedIn URL. Vantera scans your website to learn what you sell, so it can find leads that actually fit — you don't fill that in by hand.
2. **Connect your LinkedIn** — your agent runs outreach from your own LinkedIn through a secure hosted login. We never see your password, and nothing sends until you approve it. (This is why your dashboard never has to ask you to connect later.)
3. **Confirm what we got** — a one-glance summary of your business plus the target audience and industry we derived from your site. Tweak anything that's off, and set your monthly revenue goal and average deal value so your dashboard can track pipeline in dollars.
4. **Find my first leads** — this sets your system live and starts the first prospect pull.

Everything you enter is stored privately in your own workspace and is never shared with other customers. You can change any of it later in **Settings → Workspace**.

## What "Find my first leads" sets up

From your answers, Vantera provisions your whole system at once — no wizards, nothing to deploy by hand:

- a **Prospect Agent** that sources and qualifies people who match your target audience,
- an **Outreach Agent** that writes a personal message for each one, and
- an **Intent Agent** that watches LinkedIn for people showing they're in-market — its watchlist auto-filled from your business.

It starts working within about 15 minutes, and your dashboard shows **"Finding your first prospects"** while it sources and scores your first leads. **Nothing sends until you approve it.** You can fine-tune any of it — your outreach call-to-action, targeting, intent watchlist, content — anytime from the **Agents** page.
