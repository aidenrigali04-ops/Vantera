# Getting started with Vantera

Setup takes about two minutes and asks only for what Vantera can't work out on its own. The
progress bar starts at 25% the moment you arrive — your account already exists — and fills in
three short steps.

## 1. Your details

- **Full name** — how you appear to teammates and on your own dashboard.
- **Brand name** — pre-filled from sign-up; this becomes your workspace name.
- **Website** — pre-filled if you typed it on the home page. As soon as it's in, your site's
  icon appears next to the field: that's Vantera confirming it found you.

Vantera reads your website to learn what you sell — your offerings, value propositions, and
the space you operate in — and drafts your **ideal customer profile** from it: the job titles,
industries, company sizes, and buying signals that define who your agents look for. You never
have to describe your audience by hand. You can refine all of it later in **Settings**.

## 2. Connect LinkedIn

Connect the LinkedIn account your outreach will come from. You sign in through LinkedIn's
own flow, including two-factor — your password never touches Vantera. Connecting only looks:
nothing is sent, liked, or changed until you approve it, and new or quiet accounts ramp up
gradually to stay well inside LinkedIn's limits.

## 3. Choose your plan

Pick a plan and start your **free trial**. A card is required to start, but nothing is charged
until the trial ends, and you can cancel anytime before then. The moment your trial starts,
Vantera provisions your whole system at once — no wizards, nothing to deploy by hand:

- a **Prospect Agent** that sources and qualifies people against your drafted profile,
- an **Outreach Agent** that writes a personal message for each qualified person, and
- an **Intent Agent** that watches LinkedIn for people showing they're in-market — its
  watchlist auto-filled from your business.

Your dashboard opens as soon as that's done. The agents start working within about 15 minutes.
**Nothing sends until you approve it** — every message waits in your review queue.

## Common questions

- **I closed the tab mid-setup.** Sign back in and you return to exactly the step you were on.
- **My website didn't load.** Setup continues anyway; your profile starts broad and the next
  agent run re-reads your site. You can also describe your audience in **Settings**.
- **I picked the wrong plan.** Change it anytime from **Settings → Billing**; the trial carries
  over.
- **Why is a card required?** It lets your trial convert without interrupting your agents —
  and you're never charged during the trial.
