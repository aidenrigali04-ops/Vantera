# Analytics — what your outreach is producing

**Analytics** is a tab on your Results page. It shows what your outreach is producing, counted from your own prospects, replies, and meetings. Counts come first: the funnel and the source table appear as soon as you have one prospect, with or without a deal size or a plan. Dollar figures sit underneath and need two things before they show (see below).

Until your agents have found anyone, the tab says so and links to **Set up your pipeline**.

## From found to closed

The funnel has eight stages, top to bottom. Each one counts people, not messages:

- **Found** — the prospects in your workspace, however they arrived.
- **Qualified** — prospects that passed your quality bar.
- **Contacted** — prospects Vantera sent a message to or sent a connection invite to.
- **Accepted** — prospects who accepted your connection request.
- **Replied** — prospects who replied at all.
- **Interested** — prospects who sent an interested reply.
- **Meetings** — prospects with a booked meeting. A closed deal counts here too, even if no meeting was recorded, because it got past that point.
- **Closed** — prospects you marked as won.

The small percentage beside each count is that stage as a share of the stage above it.

A stage can be bigger than the one above it. When that happens the funnel says why: "A stage can exceed the one before it when a prospect replied or booked without an invite from Vantera." For example, someone you already knew might reply to a message you sent yourself.

## Where prospects come from

Below the funnel, the **Where prospects come from** table breaks the same people down by how they reached you. Each row has the columns **Found**, **Contacted**, **Accepted**, **Replied**, **Interested**, and **Closed**, with the same meanings as the funnel.

- **Signals** — one row per kind of buying signal captured on your prospects, such as a funding round or an executive hire. The biggest rows come first.
- **Intent watchlist** — people your Intent Agent surfaced, grouped by what they were reacting to: **Keyword mentions**, **Competitor engagers**, **Creator engagers**, and **Hashtag activity**. **Removed watch targets** holds people surfaced by something you have since taken off the watchlist, so they are still counted.
- **Other** — **No signal captured** is everyone who arrived with no signal and no watchlist match.
- **Agents** — the same people by who brought them in: **Prospect agent**, **Intent agent**, and **Added by you** (prospects you added, imported, or received yourself). The **Outreach agent** row counts outreach across all your prospects. Its Found cell shows a dash, because the Outreach Agent reaches people but doesn't find them.

"A prospect with several signals counts once in each row." So someone with a funding round and an executive hire appears in both rows, and the signal rows can add up to more than your Found total. The Prospect agent, Intent agent, and Added by you rows don't overlap: each prospect counts in only one of them.

On a very large workspace, the funnel and the table both read up to 5,000 records of each kind, so their counts can stop short of your full totals. When that limit is reached, the note under the table adds "Showing the first 5,000 rows."

## Dollars: return on spend

The dollar figures appear only once you have **both** an average deal size and an active plan:

- **Return on spend** — your pipeline plus closed revenue, divided by what your plan costs for a year.
- **Cost / meeting** and **Cost / close** — what each booked meeting and each closed deal costs at your current plan price. Each shows a dash until you have one.
- **Annual spend** — what your current plan costs for a year.
- **Pipeline-to-spend** — a bar toward **2×**. Pipeline worth at least twice your annual spend is the level that keeps a budget funded, and the line under the bar tells you whether you're above or below it.

If one of the two is missing, you see a single line naming it instead: "Set your average deal size to see pipeline and return in dollars." (links to Settings) or "Choose a plan to see return on spend." (links to Billing). You can also set your deal size from Today when it asks. Closed revenue uses the deal values you type when you close a deal, and your average deal size fills in when a deal has none. That way Analytics and your Overview always show the same closed number.
