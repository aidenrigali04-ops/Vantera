# Analytics and return on spend

Analytics answers one question: is your spend paying off? It's the page to open before a renewal decision.

- **Return on spend** — your pipeline-to-spend ratio: the value of everything you've closed plus your expected pipeline, divided by what your plan costs for a year. The bar to clear is **2×** — pipeline worth at least twice your annual spend is the level that keeps a budget funded. The card tells you whether you're above or below it.
- **Cost per meeting** and **cost per close** — what each booked meeting and each closed deal costs you at your current plan price. They show a dash until you've booked a meeting / closed a deal, so the number is always real.
- **Conversion funnel** — how leads move from **Qualified** (passed the quality gate) → **Contacted** → **Replied** → **Meetings** → **Closed**, with the conversion rate between each stage. Each stage shows a **typical range** for quality outreach next to your rate, so a low count reads correctly: what matters is the *rate*, not the volume. "On track" means you're in the healthy band; "room to improve" flags the one stage worth your attention. A sharp drop at one stage tells you where to focus.

**To see dollar figures**, set your **average value per client** in Settings — until then the page shows counts, not dollars. The ratio and cost-per figures also need an active plan, since they measure pipeline against what you pay.

**Meetings tracking** turns on once your AI caller books a meeting; until then that stage reads zero with a note, so nothing on the page is a guess.
