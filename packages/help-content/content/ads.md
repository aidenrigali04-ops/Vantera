# Ads & lead nurturing

The Ads workspace turns your offer into ready-to-run ad concepts and routes the leads they bring in straight into your nurture sequence. You describe what you're offering and who it's for; Vantera writes a few on-brand concepts — headline, primary text, a call-to-action, and a brief for the visual — grounded in what your business actually does.

## Generating concepts

1. **Name the campaign** — how you'll recognize it later.
2. **Offer** — what the ad promotes: a free teardown, a trial, a benchmark report.
3. **Target** — who it's for. If you've deployed a Prospect Agent, your first ICP is suggested automatically so your ads and your outbound aim at the same people.
4. **Goal** — what a click should lead to.
5. **How many concepts** — generate up to five at once, each a different angle on the same offer.

Every concept is written from real context about your business. If a concept ever makes a claim we can't ground in what you told us — a stat or result you didn't provide — it's flagged for your review instead of being treated as ready. Honest copy is the point: fabricated proof is exactly what makes buyers distrust ads.

## Reviewing concepts

Open a campaign to see its concepts. Each shows the headline, primary text, call-to-action, and a creative brief you can hand to a designer or a generator. Flagged concepts carry a note about what to fix before they run.

## Publishing and creative

Connecting your ad account and generating the visuals are set up for your workspace once your account is linked. Until then, you can copy any concept directly into your ad manager — the important part already works: any lead who fills out the ad's form flows back into Vantera automatically.

## What happens to ad leads

A lead who submits your ad form is added as a lead from ads and enrolled into your nurture sequence — the same engine that nurtures the rest of your pipeline. As always, anyone on your suppression list is never contacted, even if they fill out a form. You'll see ad-sourced leads alongside the rest under Leads, and their progress under Pipeline and Analytics.
