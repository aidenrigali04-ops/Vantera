# Signing in

You can sign in with **Continue with Google** or with the email and password you registered with. Google and email logins open the same account when they share an email address. New accounts are ready immediately — creating an account signs you in and takes you straight to setup, no confirmation email to wait for.

## Continue with Google

On the sign-in or create-account page, choose **Continue with Google**, pick the Google account you want to use, and you'll land in your workspace (or setup, if you're new). If Google sign-in isn't offered, use email and password instead.

## Forgot your password?

Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page. If an account exists for the email you enter, you'll receive a reset link. The link signs you in and asks for a new password. This also works if you originally created the account with Google and later want a password.

## Link expired?

Password-reset links are single-use and expire. Request a fresh one from the forgot-password page.
