# Vantera — 2-Week LinkedIn Content Calendar (built 2026-07-18)

Designed to optimize three things at once:
- **Marketing** — a narrative arc that manufactures demand (problem → wedge → proof) and differentiates on safety + qualification, not volume.
- **Outreach** — every post is a reason to DM and a hook to reference; the daily routine feeds warm inbound into the [DM scripts](2026-07-18-first-users-gtm-plan.md).
- **Scaling** — content compounds at $0 and ends in a founding-member conversion post + case-study engine that scales past 1:1.

**Voice = the copy brain's voice.** Plain, warm, specific. No dashes as punctuation, no business-speak (leverage, streamline, game-changer, "reach out," "excited to connect"), at most one exclamation, one thought per line. Read it back as a text. If it sounds like marketing, cut it.

**Rules that move the algorithm (grounded best practice):**
- Hook lives in the first 1–2 lines (before the "see more" cut). Earn the click.
- **No external links in the post body** — links suppress reach. Put the booking link in the first comment or move it to DM.
- Reply to every comment in the first 60 minutes. Ask a question at the end so there's something to reply to.
- One post per weekday, same ~time (pick the 1–2 hour window your audience is active, usually weekday mornings).
- Comment on 5–10 ICP posts *before* you post — it warms your reach and puts you in front of buyers.

## Week 1 — Problem + Wedge (create demand, differentiate)

### Day 1 · Mon · Text · *the ban angle*
**Hook:** The fastest way to kill your LinkedIn isn't spam. It's a tool sending "safe" volume on your behalf while you sleep.
**Body:** Most automation optimizes for the one number that gets you restricted: sends per week. 300, 500, 800 invites. That's not growth, it's a countdown. The accounts that survive send fewer, sharper touches inside real limits. I'd rather book 3 right conversations than blast 300 people and lose an account I spent years building.

**Why:** Fear + a hot take = comments. Opens the wedge on day one.

**Hook:** Honest question for anyone doing outbound on LinkedIn.
**Poll:** Biggest LinkedIn outreach problem? → Low reply rates / Takes too much time / Account safety / Finding the right people
**Body (2 lines):** Whatever you picked, the root cause is usually the same. We were taught to do more. The real move is to do less, to the right people.
**CTA:** Vote, then tell me which one actually costs you the most.
**Why:** Polls get outsized reach and hand you real audience data to aim future posts and DMs.

### Day 4 · Thu · Carousel · *value, saveable*
**Hook (slide 1):** 5 signs your LinkedIn tool is about to get you restricted.
**Slides:** 1) It brags about volume. 2) Same message to everyone. 3) No ramp or warm-up. 4) Robotic send intervals. 5) You can't see what it sends before it sends. Final slide: the fix — qualify first, cap the sends, approve every message.
**CTA:** Save this before your next campaign. Which one bit you?
**Why:** Saveable checklists travel. It frames the wedge as a standard, not a pitch.

## The daily operating routine (this is what actually converts)

Content without this is just posting. 30–45 min/day:
1. **Before you post (15 min):** leave a real comment on 5–10 posts from your ICP + people you want to reach. Warms your reach and puts your name in front of buyers.
2. **Post**, then **reply to every comment in the first 60 min.** Turn any warm commenter into a DM.
3. **DM every relevant new follower / connection** with the light opener from the plan. Content earns the follow, the DM starts the conversation.
4. **Route inbound** ("show me", "founding", "switch", teardown requests) straight into the [warm/cold DM scripts + booking link](2026-07-18-first-users-gtm-plan.md).
5. **Screenshot strong comments and wins** — they become tomorrow's proof posts and case studies (the scaling flywheel).

## What to measure (north star = conversations, not likes)
Track weekly: inbound DMs, "show me / founding / switch" replies, demos booked, connection requests received, profile views. Likes and impressions are only useful as leading indicators of those.

## How it compounds into scale
- Week 1 builds the audience's belief (problem + wedge). Week 2 cashes it in (proof + invite).
- Every post is reusable as a cold-DM reference ("saw your comment on my post about account safety").
- Wins from the founding cohort become case-study posts, which feed the next cohort. That is the $0 scaling loop.

## Honesty guardrails (non-negotiable)
- No invented metrics or fake anecdotes. Day 6 posts only after a real product-sourced demo. Day 5 teardown uses real DMs (blur names).
- Keep prod funded — the dogfood engine behind Day 6 and Day 8 runs on the same Anthropic workspace as everything else.
