It brags about volume

"500 invites a week" is a selling point for the tool and a red flag for LinkedIn. High volume is the exact pattern that gets accounts flagged.

Why it flags youSend velocity is the first thing LinkedIn watches for.

One message, everyone

If the same template hits 300 people, reply rates crater and spam reports climb. Both are signals LinkedIn reads as automation.

Why it flags youIdentical text at scale looks like a bot, because it is one.

No ramp, no warm-up

A new account that starts blasting on day one looks nothing like a real person. Trust is built slowly, then spent slowly.

You can't see what it sends

Why it flags youNo approval step means no brakes.

07 / 08

The fix

It's not a better tool. It's a different rule.

1
Qualify firstContact fewer, better people. Skip the rest.
2
Cap the sendsStay inside safe limits, always, even when you could push.
3
Approve every messageNothing goes out that isn't yours.

Safety isn't the tradeoff for growth. It's how the growth lasts.
